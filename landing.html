What is it

A website built to convert,
not just to look good

A landing page is a single focused webpage with one clear purpose — get the visitor to take one specific action. No menus pulling them away, no other pages to browse. Just your offer, and a button.

Unlike a full website, a landing page is built around a campaign or goal: generate phone enquiries, collect WhatsApp leads, promote a new project launch, sell a single product or service.

Every element — the headline, the layout, the images, the call-to-action button — is designed to guide the visitor toward that one action. That's why landing pages typically convert far better than generic website homepages.

We build landing pages that are fast, mobile-first, and ready to go live within 48 hours. You provide the details about your business, we handle everything else.

Questions

Frequently asked

How is a landing page different from a normal website?
A landing page has one page and one goal — get the visitor to take one specific action. A normal website has multiple pages and serves many purposes. Landing pages are purpose-built to convert; websites are built to inform and explore.
Do I need a domain name before we start?
No. We can host the page on a subdomain for free, or connect your existing domain if you have one. We'll guide you through the domain setup at no extra charge.
How long does it actually take?
Most landing pages are ready for your first review within 24–48 hours of us receiving your brief and content. After revisions, we typically go live within 3–5 days total.
What do you need from me to get started?
Just fill out the brief form — your business name, what the page is for, any logo or brand colours you have, and any reference sites you like. We'll handle the rest. If you have photos or product images, share those too.
Can I update the page content later?
Yes. You can message us any time with text or image updates and we'll make the changes. For ongoing clients we're usually very quick to turnaround small edits.
Is this the right option if I'm just starting out?
Absolutely. A landing page is often the smartest first step — low cost, fast to build, and gives you a professional online presence immediately. You can always expand to a full website later.
